Preparation

Before you get in touch

A few details help us understand the job from the start.

01

The job

Know what you need: for example a new charger, an upgraded fuse box, testing, or a fault to be traced.

02

What you have noticed

For a fault, note when it happens, which rooms or circuits are affected, and whether a breaker has tripped.

03

Safety first

If there is a burning smell, scorching or immediate danger, switch off at the consumer unit only if it is safe to do so, and call 999 if anyone is at risk.
